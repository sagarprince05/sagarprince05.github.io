How I work

The forward-deployed loop

Get close to the problem, build against real data, ship into the real environment, keep improving from real usage.

01

Discover

I sit with the people who own the problem and map how the work really happens — not how the process document says it does.

  • Business & technical requirements
  • Find where time and trust are lost
  • Agree what "working" means
02

Build

Prototype fast against real data and APIs, show a working sample, then harden it into a production system with safety nets.

  • AI / LLM apps, agents & automations
  • APIs, databases, third-party services
  • Sample-before-deploy sign-off
03

Deploy & iterate

Ship into the customer's environment, watch it run, hunt the silent failures, keep improving from what users actually do.

  • Cloud deployment & CI/CD
  • Debugging, reliability, cost
  • Plain-English stakeholder updates
