About

I'm an AI deployment engineer who turns messy, manual processes into systems that run themselves. I design the workflow, build the AI or automation behind it, ship it to production and keep it honest — with Claude Code as my daily pair programmer.

In a two-month internship at KGeN I shipped four production systems for Humyn Labs that teams now use every day: a site onboarding & approval workflow, a Slack assistant that answers questions about live operations data in ~3 seconds, a competitor price-intelligence pipeline and a receipt-logging Telegram bot — all running on free-tier infrastructure at ₹0/month. Along the way I also built an Android launcher platform with four branded apps and an ATS CV-scoring tool.

I care about systems that never invent data, fail loudly instead of silently, are secure by default, and are simple enough for the business to change without touching code.
