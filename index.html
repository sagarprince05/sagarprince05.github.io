How I work

Here's how it works

The forward-deployed loop: get close to the problem, build with real data, ship into the real environment, and keep improving from real usage.

01

Discover

I sit with the people who own the problem — field teams, ops leads, approvers — and map how the work really happens, not how the org chart says it does.

  • Business & technical requirements
  • Find where time and trust are lost
  • Agree what "working" means
02

Build

Prototype fast against the real data and APIs, show a working sample, then harden it into a production system with audits and safety nets.

  • AI / LLM apps, agents & automations
  • APIs, databases, third-party services
  • Sample-before-deploy sign-off
03

Deploy & iterate

Ship into the customer's environment, watch it run, hunt the silent failures, and keep improving from what users actually do.

  • Cloud & on-prem deployment
  • Debugging, reliability, cost
  • Plain-English updates to stakeholders
