Approval Workflow Automation

An internal web-form and email approval system that replaced a manual, spreadsheet-and-email sign-off process for an operations team — from first submission to final approval, with automatic notifications and a live status dashboard.

The problem

A two-stage approval process ran on spreadsheets, email threads and manual follow-up. Approvers were chased by hand, decisions were typed into sheets, nobody was notified automatically, records didn't match between the two stages, and there was no single view of where any request stood.

What I built

  • Two responsive web forms whose fields, labels, required flags and dropdowns are all driven by a configuration sheet — so the team can change the forms without touching code.
  • Automatic reference IDs — sequential, never duplicated, issued in about three seconds and sent only to the submitter. The second form opens only with a valid ID and auto-fills the record's details.
  • One-click email approvals. Approvers get a personal, signed link; whoever answers first settles it, and forged or forwarded links are rejected.
  • A review page that shows the request's details with inline media, and makes an invalid decision impossible to record (approve and reject are mutually exclusive).
  • Automatic notifications to every stakeholder at each decision, plus a login-protected live dashboard showing status, approver and timestamps.

How I worked

  • Sample before deploy. Every change was previewed as a working sample and signed off by stakeholders before going live.
  • Zero-disruption rollouts. Links and emails issued before a change kept working after it.
  • Hunting silent failures. I found and fixed submissions rejected by hidden required fields, answers dropped by a missing backend column, and notifications skipped when a decision bypassed the workflow — then recovered the messages that had been missed.
  • Security review. Signed per-person links, dashboard authentication, and an access audit that tightened who could open the underlying data.
